# LOBB brand guidelines

## Asset selection

| Context | Asset |
| --- | --- |
| Small product icon | `logo/mark.svg` |
| Light surface | `logo/lockup-light.svg` or `logo/wordmark-dark.svg` |
| Dark surface | `logo/lockup-dark.svg` or `logo/wordmark-light.svg` |
| Browser and PWA icon | `icons/favicon.svg` / `icons/maskable-512.png` |
| Trust label | `badges/verified.svg` |

## Placement

- Minimum mark size: 16px. Minimum wordmark/lockup height: 24px.
- Keep clear space equal to the ball diameter around every logo asset.
- Do not redraw, rotate, crop, outline, shadow, or recolour the mark.
- Clay is reserved for the trajectory and ball; use it sparingly in surrounding UI.

## Delivery

All SVG logo assets contain paths only and do not depend on installed fonts. The `public/` favicon and PWA icons are the deployed copies; `brand/` is the source-of-truth asset kit.
